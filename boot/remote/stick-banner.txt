
  LucinaOS recovery stick.

    Type   lucina-recover   and press Enter.

  It restores the loader and goes back to the previous generation, and says what it did. Nothing else on the disk changes.
  lucina-recover check     says what it would do, writes nothing.
  lucina-recover loader    only the loader: keeps the generation.
  lucina-recover old       the same with the previous loader, if the first did not help.
